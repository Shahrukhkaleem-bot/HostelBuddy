## 5️⃣ **RATING IMPACT ON SEARCH RANKING**

### Search Algorithm Enhancement:
```
Ranking Formula:
Rank Score = (Rating × 0.4) + (Recency × 0.3) + (Reviews × 0.2) + (Price Match × 0.1)

Where:
- Rating: Hostel's overall rating (normalized 0-1)
- Recency: Recent reviews boost (last 30 days)
- Reviews: More reviews = more trustworthy
- Price Match: How close to user's budget

Examples:
High Rated (4.8) + Few Reviews = Lower rank
Good Rated (4.2) + Many Reviews = Higher rank
Average (3.5) + Trending Up = Gets boost
```

### Featured Listings:
```
4.5+ rating → 2x visibility boost
4.0+ rating → 1.5x visibility boost
<3.0 rating → Warnings shown
```
